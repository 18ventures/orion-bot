FAQ
What is Orion?

Orion is an automated trading system that opens and manages BTC positions on your behalf. You don't need to do anything day to day — this dashboard just shows you what's happening.

Why does my balance sometimes show $0?

This usually means your account hasn't been funded yet, or your funds are sitting under "Spot" on Hyperliquid instead of "Perps" — Orion can only trade with funds on the Perps side. If you've already funded your account and still see $0 after a few minutes, message Nathan.

What does "No open position" mean?

Exactly that — there's no trade running for your account right now. This is completely normal; Orion doesn't trade constantly, only when a genuine setup appears.

Where does my money actually sit?

In your own Hyperliquid account, under your own wallet — never with Nathan directly. The "agent wallet" he holds can only place trades; it can never withdraw or move your funds.

How do I add more funds or withdraw?

Both are done directly on Hyperliquid, the same way you funded originally — this dashboard is for viewing only. Message Nathan if you're unsure of the steps.

Who do I contact with questions?

Message Nathan directly — he's the person who set up your account and can help with anything this page doesn't answer.
